Understanding Compa-Ratio

What is Compa-Ratio?

Your compa-ratio, calculated by dividing your current salary by the range midpoint, indicates your position within the compensation range. This metric helps guide compensation decisions and identifies growth opportunities.

Formula:

Compa-ratio = (Your base salary (for non-sales roles) or Your OTE (for sales roles) ÷ Midpoint)

Example: 100 ÷ 100 = 1.00

Interpreting Your Compa-Ratio:

  • Below 1.0: Your position is below the market midpoint (room for growth)
  • At 1.0: Your position matches the market midpoint (competitive rate)
  • Above 1.0: Your position exceeds the market midpoint (premium compensation)
